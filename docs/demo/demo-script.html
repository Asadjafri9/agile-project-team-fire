15 minutes before

Product Owner
  • Team → Create invite link three times: Scrum Master, Developer and QA Tester, each with Use limit 1. Send each presenter their link.
  • Create one more link, for Viewer: 1 day, Use limit 1. Keep its code ready for the examiner.
  • Backlog: at least 5 items, each estimated (1, 2, 3, 5, 8 or 13 points). Give one of them a 2-item checklist.
  • Settings → WIP limit In Progress = 2, so the warning appears during the demo.
  • Sprint planning: make sure no sprint is active (complete any running one). Only one sprint can be active at a time.

Each presenter

Before the start
  • Open your invite link, then sign in or Create account. You join the project automatically.
  • Use your own laptop or a separate browser profile. Two accounts in one browser share a session.
  • Keep the project open on your scene's page (see the scenes).
  • Leave the bell in the top bar visible. Mentions arrive there within about 4 seconds.

Golden rule: say what you're about to click, click it, then point at the result. Every action is saved to the database and appears on everyone's screen within about 4 seconds, so presenters can hand over to each other without refreshing.

Product Owner2 min
Do
  1. Backlog → New item: Story “Export sprint report”, 5 points, priority High.

3

Plan and start the sprint

Scrum Master2.5 min
Do
  1. Sprint planning → New sprint: name, goal, start today, end +13 days, capacity 20 pts / 70 h → Create sprint.
  2. Add 4 backlog items, including “Export sprint report”. Watch the Commitment check fill up.
  3. Show the Before you start checklist turn green, then click Start sprint.
Point out
  • Starting is blocked until there's a goal, valid dates and only estimated items.
  • At start, the commitment is frozen. It's the baseline for the burndown.

5

Test and close the story NEW

QA Tester2.5 min
Do
  1. Click the bell → the mention. You land on that comment, highlighted in orange.
  2. Move the card to Testing. Try Done: it's refused while the checklist is open.
  3. Tick both checklist items → move to Done.
  4. Backlog → New item: type Bug, “CSV header misses timezone”, 2 points (it needs an estimate to join the active sprint).
Point out
  • Notifications open the exact comment, on the right screen.
  • Done requires the Definition of Done.
  • QA Testers have working rights, but not planning or priority.

6

Change scope, then complete the sprint

Scrum Master2 min
Do
  1. Sprint planning: Add the new bug to the active sprint. The dialog asks “Why is the active sprint changing?”, so type a reason and Move item.
  2. Complete sprint → under Move unresolved items to, pick Backlog or a planned sprint → confirm.
Point out
  • Scope changes are recorded with who, when and why. The original commitment never changes.
  • Results freeze at completion.
